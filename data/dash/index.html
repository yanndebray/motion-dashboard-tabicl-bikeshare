How the estimates work. No dock-inventory data exists for 2012. An empty-dock moment is a departure beyond what a full dock could supply: each station starts the morning at its dock count, every trip moves one bike, and each departure that would take stock below zero counts once. Those bikes had to come from a van or a lucky arrival.

The Hill is Columbia Heights, Mount Pleasant, Adams Morgan and Park View: stations above 50 m, north of the Florida Ave escarpment. Downtown is the K St, Farragut, Metro Center and Penn Quarter core. Dock counts are today's, since 2012 counts are not published. Trip totals match the UCI Bike Sharing dataset within 1.1%.
